Privacy Policy

This application is a private automation tool used by its owner to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

It accesses only the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel data, through the owner's explicit authorization. It does not collect, sell, share or store personal data of any other person. Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
